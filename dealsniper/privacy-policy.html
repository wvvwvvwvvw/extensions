DealSniper Privacy Policy

Last updated November 6, 2026

DealSniper is a browser extension for Upwork. This policy explains what it reads, what it keeps and what leaves your device.

What DealSniper reads

The Upwork job page you have open: job title, description, budget, posted date, proposal activity and the client details the page shows (such as hire rate, total spent, average rate paid, payment verification, hires, hours and rating). It reads these only on the pages it runs on, to do its job.

What DealSniper keeps

Client evaluations and job details you chose to evaluate, proposal drafts, your profile facts and writing presets, your Gemini API key, your settings, daily usage counts and your plan status. All of this stays in your browser's local extension storage on your device. The developer has no access to it.

What leaves your device

DealSniper sends nothing to its developer. There are no accounts, analytics or trackers.

When you click Generate, the job's title and description and the facts and preferences you entered go directly from your browser to Google's Gemini API (generativelanguage.googleapis.com), authorized with your own API key. Your API key also goes to Google when you save it or refresh the model list, so DealSniper can check it and list the models it can use. Google handles that data under its own terms.

Payments

Pro is in early access: choosing a plan unlocks Pro with no charge, and DealSniper collects no payment details. Before paid subscriptions start, this policy will be updated to name the payment provider and what it handles.

What DealSniper never does

  • It doesn't sell or share your data.
  • It doesn't use your data for advertising, credit decisions or anything unrelated to its purpose.
  • It doesn't run remote code.
  • DealSniper never submits proposals or takes any action on Upwork for you.

Your control

You can export or delete your saved records in the DealSniper workspace. Removing the extension deletes everything it stored in your browser.
