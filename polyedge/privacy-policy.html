PolyEdge Privacy Policy

Last updated November 6, 2026

PolyEdge is a browser extension for Polymarket. This policy explains what it reads, what it keeps and what leaves your device.

What PolyEdge reads

The address of the Polymarket event page you have open. It reads these only on the pages it runs on, to do its job.

What PolyEdge keeps

Analyses you choose to save, your settings, daily usage counts and your plan status. All of this stays in your browser's local extension storage on your device. The developer has no access to it.

What leaves your device

PolyEdge sends nothing to its developer. There are no accounts, analytics or trackers.

To run an analysis, your browser requests public market data directly from Polymarket's public services (gamma-api.polymarket.com and clob.polymarket.com), identifying only the event and market. Polymarket handles those requests under its own terms.

Payments

Pro is in early access: choosing a plan unlocks Pro with no charge, and PolyEdge collects no payment details. Before paid subscriptions start, this policy will be updated to name the payment provider and what it handles.

What PolyEdge never does

  • It doesn't sell or share your data.
  • It doesn't use your data for advertising, credit decisions or anything unrelated to its purpose.
  • It doesn't run remote code.
  • PolyEdge never connects to a wallet, sees your funds or places orders.

Your control

You can export or delete your saved records in the PolyEdge workspace. Removing the extension deletes everything it stored in your browser.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above will change too. Questions: dubsteplers@gmail.com

PolyEdge is an independent tool, not affiliated with or endorsed by Polymarket.
