SellerPulse Privacy Policy

Last updated November 6, 2026

SellerPulse is a browser extension for Amazon. This policy explains what it reads, what it keeps and what leaves your device.

What SellerPulse reads

The Amazon.com product page you have open: product title, ASIN, price, and any rating, Best Sellers Rank, category and weight the page shows. It reads these only on the pages it runs on, to do its job.

What SellerPulse keeps

Your calculation drafts and the costs you enter, scenarios you save to the Deal Sheet, daily usage counts and your plan status. All of this stays in your browser's local extension storage on your device. The developer has no access to it.

What leaves your device

SellerPulse sends nothing anywhere: not to its developer and not to any third party. There are no accounts, analytics or trackers. All calculations run on your device.

Payments

Pro is in early access: choosing a plan unlocks Pro with no charge, and SellerPulse collects no payment details. Before paid subscriptions start, this policy will be updated to name the payment provider and what it handles.

What SellerPulse never does

  • It doesn't sell or share your data.
  • It doesn't use your data for advertising, credit decisions or anything unrelated to its purpose.
  • It doesn't run remote code.
  • SellerPulse never buys, lists or changes anything on Amazon.

Your control

You can export or delete your saved records in the SellerPulse workspace. Removing the extension deletes everything it stored in your browser.

Changes and contact

SellerPulse is an independent tool, not affiliated with or endorsed by Amazon.
